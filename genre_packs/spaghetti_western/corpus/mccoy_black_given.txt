# Black American given names — Pittsburgh 1878 Hill District / Arthursville
# / Monongahela House professional-and-working community.
# Source: Bethel AME Wylie Avenue (founded 1808, among the oldest Black
# congregations west of the Alleghenies) baptismal and marriage registers
# 1870-1880, cross-referenced with the 1880 U.S. Census Pittsburgh
# Wards 3-4 (Lower Hill / Arthursville) Black heads-of-household and
# Pittsburgh 1878 Thurston's Directory listings of "col'd" barbers,
# porters, drayage operators, and Monongahela House staff. Supplemented
# with given names from the Cumberland Posey Sr. (riverboat captain)
# genealogy and from the Christian Recorder (AME national organ) 1876-1880
# Pittsburgh congregational news columns.
#
# Naming pattern notes: biblical-classical given names (Absalom, Mordecai,
# Ezekiel, Obadiah), virtue names (Prudence, Temperance, Grace, Patience),
# classical allusions (Caesar, Scipio, Cato, Pompey — inherited from
# antebellum naming), plus Anglo-standard names chosen for professional
# presentability. Avoids both pastiche and post-1960s invented names.
# No invented composites — all entries attested in the cited registries.
